## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an approachable, high-productivity in-browser web utility hub ("TOOLS WORKSPACE"). It balances functional rigor with an enthusiastic, illustrative micro-utility atmosphere. The experience dissolves the intimidating nature of technical document manipulators, image processors, and developer tooling into friendly, tactile micro-apps.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Inviting & Tactile:** Replaces sterile, monochromatic utility platforms with warm paper-tinted canvases, lively pastel card surfaces, and bouncy 2.5D visual cues.
- **Immediate & Trustworthy:** Prioritizes instant, private browser execution (no forced file uploads to remote servers) through transparent status indicators and clear tool readiness chips.
- **Curated & Playful:** Incorporates micro-illustrations, stylized vector glyphs, and candy-colored status badges that turn everyday clerical and coding chores into engaging tasks.

### Design Style
The system adopts **Illustrative Neo-Utility**—a hybrid blend of soft tactile surfaces, subtle layered ambient shadows, structured geometric borders, and colorful pill-shaped indicators. Tool modules act as individual cards, offering clean tactile hover reactions, clear informational hierarchies, and vibrant categorical accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes a warm, approachable base punctuated by vibrant accents. It avoids sterile cool grays in favor of an artisanal cream backdrop that softens eye fatigue during prolonged workflow sessions.

### Core Palettes
- **Canvas / Surface:**
  - Canvas Base: `#FAF8F5` (warm cream tinted neutral)
  - Card & Modal Background: `#FFFFFF` (crisp white for maximum contrast against the soft base)
  - Card Secondary Surface: `#F4EFEA` (recessed inputs, search bars, and deactivated states)
- **Primary Accent (`#4F46E5` - Electric Indigo):** Anchors core actions, primary interactive states, branded glyph backdrops, and active category tab pills.
- **Secondary Accent (`#FF6B6B` - Punchy Coral / Salmon):** Highlights hot tools, "NEW" (`ใหม่`) status banners, and celebratory features.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#10B981` - Mint Emerald):** Signifies live browser readiness (`ใช้งานได้`), successful operations, and active system verifications.
- **Utility & Warning Accents:**
  - Amber / Sunflower: `#F59E0B` (in-progress states `อยู่ระหว่างพัฒนา`, tips, and alert banners)
  - Sky Blue: `#0EA5E9` (preview links, auxiliary developer utilities, and quick guides)
  - Soft Lavender / Violet: `#8B5CF6` (conversion pipelines and format switches)
- **Typography & Structural Neutrals:**
  - Neutral Base / Titles: `#0F172A` (deep slate navy for crisp legibility)
  - Body & Descriptive Text: `#475569` (neutral slate gray)
  - Muted / Placeholder / Borders: `#CBD5E1` (subtle structural divider strokes)
  - Outline Tint: `#E2E8F0` / `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`

## Typography

Typography pairs high geometric clarity with organic warmth, accommodating bilingual Thai and Latin character sets seamlessly.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Headlines & Display Hero:** Uses extra-bold weights with tight negative tracking to generate punchy, editorial impact across section headers and the central hero statement.
- **Section Headers:** Accompanied by micro-accent pills or geometric diamond sparks (`◆`) to visually anchor category divisions.
- **Card Titles & Content:** Explicitly weighted between 600 and 700 to ensure quick scanning across dense multi-column tool matrices.
- **Descriptions:** Set in balanced line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) with softened slate color to provide context without stealing focus from tool titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column system, maxing out at `1280px` canvas width to preserve scan-ability and avoid card overextension on ultrawide viewports.

### Breakpoint Structure
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single column stack for hero highlights; 1 or 2 columns for micro tool cards; horizontal swipe-row for category filter chips; canvas margins pinned to `1.25rem`.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 2 or 3 columns for core tool grids; 8-column center-aligned search bar; gutter spacing `1.25rem`.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 4 uniform columns for core tool modules; 3 columns for highlighted hero tools; gutters scaled to `1.5rem`.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Category Stacks:** Sections maintain `3rem` to `3.5rem` vertical spacing, reinforced by micro-section headers and horizontal separator rules.
- **Card Padding:** Padded symmetrically at `1.25rem` (`space-lg`) on desktop and `1rem` on mobile, keeping content compact while offering breathing room for multi-line Thai descriptions.

## Elevation & Depth

The design relies on soft, multi-stop diffusion shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphic shading, preserving a clean illustrative paper look.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Flat `#FAF8F5` matte paper texture.
- **Resting Tool Cards (Level 1):** Solid white surface bordered by a crisp `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, layered over a soft drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Hovered / Interactive Cards (Level 2):** Elevated via a gentle vertical lift (`translateY(-3px)`), bordered by `1px solid rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.25)`, with a luminous colored shadow: `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Filter Pills & Interactive Controls:** Flat solid fills or ghost outlines that elevate slightly with an inner subtle glow when toggled active.
- **Feature Top-Stripe Ribbons:** High-saturation colored accent bars (e.g. Salmon `#FF6B6B` or Indigo `#4F46E5`) pinned directly flush to the top border of the card to indicate distinct statuses like "New" or "In Development".

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses balanced, friendly curves that soften utility interfaces without appearing childish.

### Radius Scale
- **Small Elements (Pills, Micro Tags, Checkboxes):** `9999px` full pill shapes or `0.375rem` rounded corners.
- **Tool Glyphs & Illustrated Badges:** `0.75rem` to `1rem` squircle enclosures with pastel tinted backgrounds.
- **Cards & Surface Panels:** Standardized to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), creating a unified, welcoming card collection.
- **Search & Primary Inputs:** `9999px` full capsules for global search inputs, reinforcing the modern consumer-grade feel.

## Components

### 1. Tool Cards
- **Structure:** White card container with an optional top color ribbon (3px tall) for tagged statuses.
- **Header:** Houses a 44x44px rounded tool glyph container on the left with a subtle pastel background corresponding to the tool type, alongside a contextual status pill (`ใช้งานได้` in mint, `ใหม่` in coral, `อยู่ระหว่างพัฒนา` in gray/amber) on the top right.
- **Content:** Tool name in bold `title-sm` followed by a concise, two-line utility description in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).
- **Footer Interaction:** A lightweight tap trigger (`✓ กดเพื่อใช้งาน` or `อยู่ระหว่างพัฒนา`) in muted font that transitions to primary indigo on hover.

### 2. Search & Command Capsule
- **Container:** Rounded pill container with `0 6px 24px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`, centered on the hero canvas.
- **Iconography:** Minimalist search lens on the left; keyboard shortcut pill (`⌘K`) on the right.
- **Input Style:** Clean typography with placeholder text set in soft slate (`#94A3B8`).

### 3. Category Filter Chips
- **Resting:** Ghost pill with a delicate border (`#E2E8F0`), `#FFFFFF` background, and dark slate text.
- **Hover:** Border shifts to electric indigo with an ultra-light purple wash (`#EEF2FF`).
- **Active:** Solid dark charcoal or electric indigo background (`#0F172A` or `#4F46E5`), with crisp white typography.

### 4. Status Tags & Badges
- **Ready (`ใช้งานได้`):** Soft emerald tint background (`#ECFDF5`), deep emerald text (`#065F46`), accompanied by a green micro dot.
- **New Feature (`ใหม่`):** Vibrant coral banner or pill (`#FF6B6B`) with white text.
- **In Development (`อยู่ระหว่างพัฒนา`):** Subtle neutral tint (`#F1F5F9`) with slate label (`#64748B`).

### 5. Hero Feature Cards (Top Row)
- **Visuals:** Larger cards styled with distinct tinted backgrounds (e.g. pale lavender, pale cyan, soft warm sand), equipped with external link/open diagonal arrow icons (`↗`) to represent standalone workspaces.
